Welcome,

This guide is parsed directly from the map's loot scripts. Here's how to use it:

  • Search bar — type any item name, boss, dungeon, material, or item ID. Press / to focus it, Esc to clear.
  • Filters — toggle multiple categories or source types with the chips. Narrow by level range.
  • Sortable columns — click any header to sort. Click again to reverse.
  • Drop rates — every row shows the best per-kill roll chance. Expand Breakdown for the full per-boss table.
  • Hover any item name for a full in-game style tooltip.
  • Gear Planner — click + Plan on any craftable item. Its recipe appears in the planner above, with a checkbox per material. When every material is ticked, the recipe shows Ready to be crafted!
  • Shareable links — filters and search sync to the URL.
Glossary & reading tips
Drop
A direct item-creation branch associated with that enemy in the loot script.
Craft
A registered recipe — materials listed with quantities.
Unresolved
Present in the codex and item definitions, but no drop, recipe, or shop source was found.
Trial
The item only appears when the dungeon's trial flag is active.
Mode not specified
The loot script does not gate this branch on Normal vs Trial.
Per-roll %
Effective chance for a single loot roll from a single qualifying unit.
Inner / Outer %
Inner is the chance the item is selected inside its tier. Outer is the chance the tier itself fires. Effective = inner × outer.
