Nectar Vestment — where to farm

Hall Ruins Courtyard · Normal mode
Soul Iron Golem Kurkata (Boss) and Ruined City Sage Kiriyad (Lord). Also described in the map as “Necrotic Calamity Pilgrim Hall Ruins Courtyard.”

Level requirement: 55. Item ID: hcun. Both enemies share the normal loot branch containing this item. The branch is disabled when the dungeon's trial flag is 1; the separate trial loot table does not directly create Nectar Vestment.

Chance: 30% to enter the equipment pool × 100/1700 to select Nectar Vestment = 1.7647% per roll. There are 2 rolls when the qualifying-unit count is at most 1; otherwise floor(2 + 0.75 × count). Two rolls give about 3.50% per kill for at least one vestment. The count is script-defined qualifying heroes/helper units, so do not equate it blindly with the lobby player count.

The dungeon tooltip lists equipment levels 55–65, a Divine Essence cost of 1000, and 1–2 recommended players. These are tooltip values, not a separate runtime cost audit.

How to read this guide: “Drop” means a direct item-creation branch associated with that enemy was found in the loot script. “Craft” lists the registered recipe. Shop stock and special claims are labeled separately. This is static source analysis, not an in-game test or a guarantee that every encounter and platform reward is accessible. Additional phase, eligibility, and event conditions may apply. “Mode not specified” does not mean both modes.

Coverage limits: Every codex item is listed, but two sources remain unresolved: Twisted Wand and Legendary Rune Stone: Ruined City Sage. Strigul's Bite has inconsistent material/quantity arrays in the source, flagged in its recipe. Dungeon summaries are the codex's advertised highlights and are not exhaustive loot tables. No missing drop source is inferred merely from an item's name.
